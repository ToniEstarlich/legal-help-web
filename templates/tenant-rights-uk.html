Your Rights as a Tenant in the UK

Understand basic renting laws in simple words

Basic Rights You Have

  • The right to live in a property that is safe and in good condition.
  • The right to have repairs done in a reasonable time.
  • The right to be protected from unfair eviction.
  • The right to know who your landlord is.

About Evictions

Landlords must follow a legal process to evict tenants. They must give proper notice and sometimes go to court.

What to Do If You Have a Problem

First, talk to your landlord. If they don't help, contact your local council or a housing advisor.
