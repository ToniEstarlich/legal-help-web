What Is a Contract?

A beginner-friendly explanation of contract law

Definition of a Contract

A contract is a legally binding agreement between two or more parties. It can be written or verbal, but it must include certain elements to be valid.

Key Elements of a Contract

  • Offer and Acceptance: One person offers something, and the other accepts it.
  • Consideration: Each party must give or do something of value.
  • Intention to Create Legal Relations: Both parties must intend for the agreement to be legally binding.

Why Are Contracts Important?

Contracts protect your rights. They help you prove what was agreed if something goes wrong.

For example, if you pay someone to paint your house and they don't finish the job, a contract helps you claim your rights.
