What Happens If You're Arrested in the UK?

Learn your rights and what to expect in a simple, clear way

When Can You Be Arrested?

  • If the police believe you committed a crime.
  • If they have a warrant or legal reason.
  • They must tell you why you are being arrested.

Your Basic Rights

Once arrested, you have the right to:

  • Know why you're arrested.
  • Speak to a lawyer (called a solicitor).
  • Tell someone where you are.

At the Police Station

You may be kept for up to 24 hours without charge. You can stay silent, but it's best to ask for legal advice before speaking.

After the Interview

The police may let you go, keep you under investigation, or charge you. A lawyer can help you understand what’s next.
